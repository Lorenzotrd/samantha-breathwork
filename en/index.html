FAQ

What you want to know

Does breathwork really help with anxiety?

Yes. Anxiety lives in the body too: shallow breathing, tension, a racing heart. By working directly on your nervous system, conscious breathing helps it leave alert mode, where the mind alone can't.

Do online sessions really work?

Yes. You lie down at home with headphones, and I guide you with my voice throughout the session. The work happens inside you and needs no physical contact. And after an emotional release, you stay calm at home instead of having to drive back.

Is breathwork intense?

It can bring strong emotional releases, but nothing is forced. You are guided at your own pace, in a safe setting, and you stay in control at every moment: we can slow down or pause whenever you want.

How soon will I feel the effects?

Many women feel calmer after the very first session. Lasting change in chronic anxiety takes repetition, which is why I offer session packs and a 10-session program.

Are there any contraindications?

Yes. Breathwork is not recommended if you have cardiovascular conditions, epilepsy, glaucoma or severe psychiatric conditions, or if you are pregnant. We go through all of this during the discovery call, and I won't offer a session if a contraindication applies. Breathwork complements medical care, it doesn't replace it.

Which time zones do you work in?

Sessions are fully online, in English or French, for women in Australia and Europe. When you book, available time slots are shown in your own time zone.
