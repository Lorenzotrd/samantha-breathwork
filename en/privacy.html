Privacy policy

What I do with your information, simply.

Last updated 9 October 2026

This page explains simply what I do with your information. It follows the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the GDPR (European Union).

Who is responsible

Samantha Trichard (Samantha Breathwork), Brisbane, Australia. Contact: samanthabreathwork@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • Your contact details (name, email, phone) when you book a call or a session.
  • The payment details I need (card payments go through Stripe, I never see your card number).

Why

Only to organise and tailor your sessions, contact you and keep my accounts. I never sell or share your information for marketing.

Session confidentiality

What you share in a session stays between us. Zoom sessions are not recorded and I don't take notes.

Tools I use

Calendly (booking), Zoom (video), Stripe or bank transfer (payment), Gmail and WhatsApp (messages), Vercel (website hosting). Some are based in the United States.

How long I keep it

3 years after our work together ends, then deleted. Invoices are kept for 5 years, as the law requires.

Cookies

The site may use analytics cookies. You can clear or block them at any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing samanthabreathwork@gmail.com. I reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the OAIC (Australia) or the CNIL (France).
