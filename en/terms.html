Prices, payment, packs, cancellations and your consumer rights, in Australia and in Europe.

Last updated 9 October 2026

These terms apply to every session and program booked with Samantha Breathwork. By booking a paid session, you agree to them.

1. About me

Samantha Trichard, trading as Samantha Breathwork, sole trader based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. ABN being issued. Email: samanthabreathwork@gmail.com. Phone and WhatsApp: 0412 781 419. Full details are in the legal notice.

2. What I offer

One-on-one 90-minute breathwork sessions, online only, by video on Zoom: single sessions, 3-session and 5-session packs, and the 12-session Transformation program. The 30-minute discovery call is free with no obligation.

Breathwork is not medical treatment or psychotherapy and does not replace care from your GP or a mental health professional. Before your first session, you confirm that none of the contraindications apply to you (pregnancy, epilepsy, cardiovascular conditions, glaucoma, severe psychiatric conditions and so on), or that you have checked with your doctor. If in doubt, I may decline or postpone a session for your safety.

3. Prices and payment

Prices are shown on the pricing section, in Australian dollars (AUD) or euros (EUR). The currency is the one chosen at booking and stays the same afterwards. I am not registered for GST, so no GST is added.

Payment is by card (through Stripe) or bank transfer, before the session or, for a pack or program, before its first session. Any currency conversion or bank fees charged by your bank are yours to pay.

4. How long packs last

  • 3-session pack: use within 3 months of the first session.
  • 5-session pack: use within 5 months.
  • Transformation program (12 sessions): use within 6 months.

Sessions not used by then expire, unless there is a medical reason or circumstances beyond your control, in which case I extend the period.

5. Cancelling and rescheduling

  • You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 48 hours before the session, from your Calendly confirmation or by message.
  • Within 48 hours, or if you don't show up, the session is charged (or taken from your pack).
  • If I have to cancel, you choose between a new date and a refund for that session.
  • If the connection drops on my side, we make up the time at no cost.

6. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

If you live in Australia, the consumer guarantees of the Australian Consumer Law apply: the sessions must be delivered with due care and skill and match what was described. If they don't, you can ask for the session to be redone or refunded. Nothing in these terms excludes those guarantees. There are no refunds for a simple change of mind outside the cancellation rules in section 5.

7. Clients in the European Union

If you live in the EU, you have 14 days from purchase to cancel without giving a reason. Email me at samanthabreathwork@gmail.com and I will refund you within 14 days using the same payment method. If you ask me to start a session within those 14 days, sessions already delivered are charged at the pack's per-session price, and once all sessions are delivered the right to cancel no longer applies. The French version of these terms, on the CGV page, includes a cancellation form.

8. Responsibility

I deliver each session with care and attention. I can't promise a specific result: everyone moves at their own pace. During sessions, you stay responsible for your own wellbeing: sit somewhere quiet and safe, never while driving, and stop the exercise if you feel unwell. Nothing in these terms limits my liability beyond what the law allows.

9. Confidentiality

What you share in sessions stays confidential. Sessions are not recorded. See the privacy policy for details.

10. Complaints and disputes

If something isn't right, please email me first at samanthabreathwork@gmail.com. I reply within 7 days and always look for a fair solution. If we can't agree, you can contact the Queensland Office of Fair Trading free of charge, or your state's consumer protection agency. In the EU, contact the European Consumer Centre in your country.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Queensland, Australia. If you live in the EU, you keep the protection of the mandatory consumer rules of your country of residence.
