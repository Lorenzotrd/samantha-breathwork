Perth questions, answered

Are sessions shown in Perth time?

Yes. When you book, the calendar detects your time zone and shows every slot in Perth time. You never have to work out the difference with Sydney or Melbourne.

Does daylight saving change anything?

Not for you. WA doesn't use daylight saving, so your session time stays the same. The east coast moves an hour from October to April, and the booking calendar handles it automatically.

I live outside Perth, in regional WA. Can I join?

Yes. Sessions are online, so all you need is a stable internet connection, headphones and a quiet place to lie down. It works the same in Fremantle, Joondalup or a regional town.

How much does a session cost?

A first 90-minute session is $150 AUD. Packs bring it down to $130 a session for 3 ($390) and $120 a session for 5 ($600). The 12-session Transformation program is $1,500. The 30-minute call is free.

Is breathwork a replacement for therapy?

No. It helps calm your nervous system and can sit alongside therapy, but it is not a treatment. Your GP can prepare a Mental Health Treatment Plan for Medicare-subsidised psychology sessions. Breathwork is not recommended during pregnancy or with epilepsy, heart conditions, glaucoma or severe psychiatric conditions.

If you're struggling right now

Breathwork is not a crisis service. If you are in distress, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 (free, 24/7). In an emergency, call 000. In Perth, the Mental Health Emergency Response Line is on 1300 555 788.
